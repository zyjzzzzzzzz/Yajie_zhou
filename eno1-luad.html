Ongoing project summary

ENO1-associated metabolic epithelial states in LUAD

This page is designed as a short, PI-readable research summary rather than a full manuscript.

Research question

How are mitophagy-associated metabolic programs organized across malignant epithelial states and spatial tumor niches in lung adenocarcinoma?

Approach

  • Bulk RNA-seq survival modeling and external validation.
  • WGCNA and pathway integration to prioritize candidate hub genes.
  • scRNA-seq integration, epithelial subclustering, and pseudotime analysis.
  • Spatial transcriptomics deconvolution and spatial co-localization assessment.
  • CellChat-based ligand–receptor inference as hypothesis generation.

Key findings

  • ENO1 emerged as a candidate hub linking mitophagy-associated transcriptional programs and metabolic reprogramming.
  • ENO1 was enriched in an epithelial state associated with glycolysis, hypoxia, and MYC-target signatures.
  • Spatial transcriptomics suggested co-localization of ENO1-high regions with a specific epithelial tumor niche.

Limitations and next steps

  • Current evidence is primarily computational and association-based.
  • Malignant epithelial annotation should be strengthened using CNV-based inference.
  • Spatial transcriptomics requires orthogonal validation such as multiplex IF or RNAscope.
  • CellChat and drug-sensitivity analyses should be interpreted as hypothesis-generating.
  • Future experiments could test ENO1 perturbation effects on mitophagy flux, glycolysis, mitochondrial stress, and tumor–stromal interactions.
