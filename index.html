Ongoing manuscript · primary PhD application project

ENO1-associated metabolic epithelial states in lung adenocarcinoma

Research focus: To investigate how ENO1-associated metabolic programs shape malignant epithelial states and spatial tumor microenvironment remodeling in lung adenocarcinoma.

This project integrates bulk transcriptomics, survival modeling, WGCNA, single-cell RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, pseudotime analysis, CellChat, and RT-qPCR validation to characterize ENO1-associated malignant cell states and their spatial tumor ecosystem.

  • Methods: TCGA/GEO analysis, WGCNA, survival modeling, scRNA-seq integration, spatial transcriptomics, CellChat, and RT-qPCR.
  • Key findings: ENO1 emerged as a central metabolic regulator associated with malignant epithelial states and metabolic reprogramming.
  • Biological insight: ENO1-high epithelial states showed glycolytic, hypoxic, and spatial niche-associated features.
  • Next step: strengthen malignant-cell inference and validate ENO1-associated mechanisms through functional experiments.

My role: designed and implemented the computational workflow, performed data integration and downstream analyses, interpreted results, and contributed to manuscript preparation.

Published project

TLS-related immunogenetic subtypes in lung adenocarcinoma

Research focus: To define tertiary lymphoid structure-related molecular subtypes in LUAD and evaluate their prognostic, immunological, and translational significance.

In this study, I constructed an integrated framework combining Mendelian randomization, machine-learning survival modeling, immune microenvironment profiling, tumor mutation burden analysis, single-cell RNA-seq, and RT-qPCR validation to characterize TLS-related heterogeneity in lung adenocarcinoma.

  • Methods: Mendelian randomization, machine learning, immune infiltration analysis, TMB analysis, scRNA-seq, and RT-qPCR.
  • Key findings: TLS-related immunogenetic subtypes showed distinct prognostic outcomes and tumor immune landscapes.
  • Biological insight: TLS-associated features may reflect differences in anti-tumor immunity, immune organization, and clinical risk.
  • Translational value: the work helped connect immune-structural features with prognostic stratification in LUAD.

My role: contributed to study design, performed integrated bioinformatics analyses, interpreted immune-related results, and participated in validation-oriented manuscript preparation.

Published project

BMP-related mRNA prognostic modeling in clear cell renal cell carcinoma

Research focus: To develop and externally validate a BMP-related prognostic signature in clear cell renal cell carcinoma and explore its association with the tumor microenvironment.

This project used TCGA and external validation cohorts to construct a BMP-related mRNA prognostic model for KIRC, followed by analyses of immune infiltration, tumor mutation burden, drug sensitivity, and RT-qPCR validation.

  • Methods: prognostic modeling, external validation, immune microenvironment analysis, TMB analysis, drug sensitivity analysis, and RT-qPCR.
  • Key findings: the model showed prognostic value and revealed relationships between BMP-related transcriptional signals and tumor immune features.
  • Biological insight: this work provided my foundation in translational bioinformatics and clinically relevant biomarker modeling.
  • Training value: the project strengthened my experience in end-to-end computational workflow development and biological interpretation.

My role: performed model construction, validation cohort analysis, tumor microenvironment interpretation, and contributed to figure preparation and manuscript writing.

Research maturity

Methodological Reflection

My previous projects trained me in end-to-end cancer bioinformatics, but also made me aware of the limitations of correlation-driven biomarker discovery. I am particularly interested in improving the rigor of multi-omics studies through better survival-model validation, malignant-cell annotation, spatial-statistical analysis, and experimentally testable computational hypotheses.

What I want to improve

  • Nested validation and model-selection bias control in survival ML.
  • CNV-based malignant-cell inference in single-cell cancer datasets.
  • Spatial statistics and orthogonal validation for spatial transcriptomics.
  • Careful interpretation of inferred ligand–receptor communication.
